# Kyoto Itinerary — Mon 9 Nov to Thu 12 Nov 2026

Built for Mandeep & Tom. Grouped strictly by neighbourhood to cut down transit time; heavy-walking days flagged; every restaurant/bar verified as a real, currently-operating, specialty venue (see the companion **Kyoto_Itinerary_Sources** file for citations on every claim below).

**Two corrections to the existing plan, both due to real opening-hours conflicts:**
- Kenninji closes at 16:30 (last entry) — it can't follow an afternoon Fushimi Inari visit and still be reached in time, so it's been moved to Day 3 late morning instead.
- Kyoto International Manga Museum is **closed every Wednesday** — Wed 11 Nov (the day it was implicitly grouped with Nishiki Market) doesn't work. Moved to Day 4 (Thursday, open) as an optional swap.

---

### Day 1: Arrival — Fushimi Inari & Southern Higashiyama
*   **Midday (~12:00):** Arrive Kyoto Station, drop luggage — coin lockers (¥700, large, Hachijo Exit) or Crosta Kyoto same-day delivery to your hotel (~¥1,000/bag, drop off by 2pm).
*   **Lunch:** Kyoto Ramen Koji / 京都拉麺小路 (10th floor, Kyoto Station) | *Specialty: regional ramen — 8 stalls (Sapporo miso, Hakata tonkotsu, Kyoto shoyu, etc.)* | *Review note: a curated lineup of branch stores from famous regional ramen-ya; buy tickets from the vending machine outside each stall. Convenient for an arrival-day lunch with zero detour.*
*   **Afternoon (13:30):** Fushimi Inari Taisha / 伏見稲荷大社 — lower loop up to the Yotsutsuji viewpoint (~1.5–2 hrs round trip). Free entry, open 24 hrs. *(You'll return at dawn on Day 3 for the quieter full hike to the summit — doing the lower loop now on arrival day, and the demanding upper hike when jet lag has worn off, is the better split.)*
*   **Late afternoon (~16:00):** Kiyomizu-dera / 清水寺 (¥500, open 6:00–18:00) + wander the Sannenzaka/Ninenzaka slopes below it for matcha sweets shops.
*   **Dinner:** Gion Duck Noodles / ダック ヌードルズ (Gion, 4 min from Gion-Shijo Stn) | *Specialty: duck tsukemen/ramen using Kishu duck and handmade German-rye noodles* | *Review note: 4.5/5 on Tripadvisor, ranked in Kyoto's top restaurants; small and hidden with a regular queue even in winter — go right at opening or expect a wait.*
*   **Evening (Optional):** Gion bar-hopping — L'Escamoteur (magic-themed cocktail bar, Kiyamachi-dori, opens 8pm) or Bee's Knees (Prohibition-style speakeasy, Asia's 50 Best Bars #76, walk-in only, closed Sundays).
*   **Transit/Logistics Note:** JR Nara Line, Kyoto Station → Inari Station is 5 min (local trains only — don't board an express). Keihan Main Line runs direct from Fushimi-Inari Station to Gion-Shijo (~15 min) for the move to Higashiyama.

**Backup / Rainy Day:** Kyoto National Museum (covered, right by Kiyomizu-dera) instead of the temple grounds; Nishiki Market's covered arcade also works well in rain if you swap it in from Day 4.

---

### Day 2: Arashiyama & Northwest Kyoto
*   **Morning (07:30):** % Arabica Kyoto Arashiyama / %アラビカ京都嵐山 for coffee, then the Bamboo Grove (Sagano) — go before 8:30 to beat tour groups.
*   **Late morning (08:30):** Tenryu-ji Sogenchi Garden / 天龍寺 (¥500 garden-only, opens 8:30, last entry 16:50) — one of Kyoto's finest Zen gardens.
*   **Lunch:** Sagano / ゆどうふ 嵯峨野 (just past the Bamboo Grove, near Tenryu-ji) | *Specialty: yudofu (simmered hot tofu) kaiseki-style set* | *Review note: long-running Arashiyama tofu specialist with private tatami rooms; listed on Tabelog's curated "Best Tofu/Yuba" list for the area. No reservations — arrive right at opening for lunch.*
*   **Optional add-on:** Tea Ceremony Kyoto Nagomi / 京都茶道体験 和 (Arashiyama branch) — English-friendly, book ahead online.
*   **Afternoon (13:00):** Kinkaku-ji / 金閣寺 (¥500, 9:00–17:00) then Ryoan-ji / 龍安寺 (¥600, 8:00–17:00) — an 18-min walk or 5-min bus apart.
*   **Dinner:** Pontocho Alley (various yakitori/kappo counters along the lantern-lit alley) — no single reservation needed, just walk the lane and pick a spot with a queue of locals.
*   **Evening (Optional):** Bar Rocking Chair (machiya cocktail bar near Kiyamachi, bartender Kenji Tsubokura is a former World Cocktail Champion) or Nokishita711 (reservation-only "liquid cuisine" tasting bar, seats just 4 guests per sitting — **book this one well in advance**, it's not a walk-in).
*   **Transit/Logistics Note:** This is the heaviest walking day of the trip — expect 15,000+ steps between the Bamboo Grove, Tenryu-ji, and the two NW temples. Randen (Arashiyama tram line) is the easiest way back toward central Kyoto if legs are tired.

**Backup / Rainy Day:** Swap the Bamboo Grove walk for the indoor Tenryu-ji hall + garden-from-the-veranda view, and do Kinkaku-ji/Ryoan-ji in the afternoon as planned (both are outdoor-light, short visits).

---

### Day 3: Dawn Fushimi Inari, Higashiyama Upper & Gion
*   **Early morning (07:00):** Fushimi Inari Taisha — full hike to the summit this time (quiet, ~2 hrs round trip, best light of the day).
*   **Brunch (09:00):** Vermillion Cafe (espresso bar tucked right by the shrine, open 8:30am–3pm) | *Specialty: avocado/poached-egg toast, matcha and coffee drinks* | *Review note: run by a Japanese owner who spent 18 years in Australia; consistently praised for coffee quality and blankets provided for outdoor seating.*
*   **Late morning (10:30):** Kenninji Temple / 建仁寺 (¥800, opens 10:00, last entry 16:30) — Kyoto's oldest Zen temple; the Twin Dragons ceiling painting (双龍図, 108 tatami mats) is the highlight.
*   **Midday (11:15):** Shogunzuka Seiryuden / 将軍塚青龍殿 (¥500) — a short taxi from Chion-in/Higashiyama; arguably the best panoramic view over Kyoto, and far less crowded than Kiyomizu-dera's viewing platform.
*   **Dinner:** Kichi Kichi / きっちりきっちり (Pontocho back alley) | *Specialty: theatrical tableside omurice by Chef Motokichi Yukimura* | *Review note: only 14 seats — internationally famous and **books out weeks in advance, reserve now.*** Fallback if you can't get in: Gion Duck Noodles again, or an okonomiyaki dinner at Gion Tanto (Gion Shijo, always has a line).
*   **Transit/Logistics Note:** Keihan Main Line direct from Fushimi-Inari to Gion-Shijo (~15 min) covers the whole day's transit backbone — no need to loop back through Kyoto Station.

**Backup / Rainy Day:** Kenninji and Shogunzuka's Seiryuden hall are both substantially indoor/covered; Fushimi Inari's torii tunnels give shelter for most of the hike, so this day holds up reasonably well in light rain.

---

### Day 4: Relaxed Morning, Central Kyoto & Departure
*   **Morning (08:30):** Nishiki Market / 錦市場 ("Kyoto's Kitchen") — many individual stalls close Sundays/Wednesdays, so Thursday is a good day for this; arrive before 10–11am while all stalls are open.
*   **Coffee (10:00):** Ogawa Coffee / 小川珈琲 (Kyoto's own roaster, founded 1952, one of the city's three historic coffee houses alongside Inoda and Maeda).
*   **Optional swap:** Kyoto International Manga Museum / 京都国際マンガミュージアム (¥900, 10:00–17:00, open Thursdays) — worth it only if you're willing to trim Nishiki Market short, since it needs ~1.5 hrs and you're on a departure morning.
*   **Depart (11:00–12:00):** Head to Kyoto Station for the Shinkansen to Tokyo.
*   **Transit/Logistics Note:** Keep this morning genuinely light — it's a travel day, and Day 2 already used up the trip's "heavy walking" budget.
