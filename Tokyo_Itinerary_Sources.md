# Sources — Tokyo Itinerary Plan

Every restaurant, bar, attraction, and logistics claim in `Tokyo_Itinerary_Plan` was checked against the sources below before being included, per the repo's **"NO SLOP" / zero-hallucination rule** (see `CLAUDE.md`). Compiled 31 July 2026. Where a specific opening hour/price shifts seasonally or by branch, the plan says so rather than pinning a number that might be stale — verify the exact figure on the venue's own site when booking.

## Attractions, temples, gardens & viewpoints
- **Meiji Jingu** — free entry; opens at sunrise, closes at sunset, so hours shift monthly (≈06:20–16:20 in November). Forest of ~100,000 donated trees. [Meiji Shrine hours 2026 (One Day Away)](https://onedayawaytravel.com/meiji-shrine-hours-map-history/), [official-style guide (Magical Trip)](https://www.magical-trip.com/media/the-urban-oasis-meiji-jingu-shrine/)
- **Shibuya Sky** — 229 m rooftop deck on Shibuya Scramble Square; open 10:00–22:30, last entry 21:20; ¥2,700 before 15:00 / ¥3,400 from 15:00; timed-entry tickets. [GO TOKYO official](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/spot/1749/index.html), [Trip.com ticket guide 2026](https://us.trip.com/guide/attraction/shibuya-sky-ticket.html)
- **Senso-ji (Asakusa)** — free; grounds open 24h, main hall 06:00–17:00 (06:30– Oct–Mar); Kaminarimon lantern + Nakamise-dori (~90 shops, ~10:00–19:00). [japan-guide.com](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3004.html), [JeePe guide](https://www.jeepe.jp/en/articles/senso-ji-tokyo-temple-culture-1051)
- **Tsukiji Outer Market** — 460+ stalls; most open ~05:00 and wind down by ~14:00, best window 08:00–10:00, many top stalls sell out by ~11:00; closed Sundays and scattered Wednesdays per the Tokyo Central Wholesale Market calendar. [japan-guide.com](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3021.html), [official Tsukiji site](https://www.tsukiji.or.jp/english/plattsukiji/), [Tsukiji guide 2026](https://tanuki-tabi-travel.com/blog/tsukiji-market-guide)
- **Ueno / Ameyoko / Tokyo National Museum** — Ameyoko is the open-air market street under the JR tracks; Tokyo National Museum in Ueno Park (closed Mondays; Day 4 is a Sunday, so open). [GO TOKYO — Ueno](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/spot/8/index.html)
- **Akihabara** — "Electric Town"; shops generally ~11:00–20:00, arcades later (GiGO, Taito Station, Super Potato retro games); Chuo-dori goes **car-free on Sundays, 13:00–17:00 Oct–Mar** (Day 4 is a Sunday). Kanda Myojin shrine ~7-min walk. [GO TOKYO — Akihabara](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/spot/55/index.html), [Akihabara guide 2026 (Magical Trip)](https://www.magical-trip.com/media/akihabara-through-the-seasons-a-complete-guide-to-tokyos-electric-otaku-town/)
- **teamLab Planets TOKYO (Toyosu)** — barefoot wade-through digital art; open 08:30–22:00, last entry 60 min before close; tickets from ¥3,800; **timed tickets for November go on sale ~late-August and sell out**; exhibit expanded in early 2026 (Athletic Forest / Future Park); run **extended to end of 2027**. [teamLab official](https://www.teamlab.art/e/planets/), [Tokyo Cheapo 2026 guide](https://tokyocheapo.com/entertainment/museums-and-exhibitions/everything-you-need-to-know-about-teamlab-planets/), [Trip.com tickets 2026](https://us.trip.com/travel-guide/attraction/tokyo/teamlab-planets-tokyo-61909015/)
- **Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building observation decks** — Nishi-Shinjuku, 202 m, **free**; nightly "Tokyo Night & Light" projection-mapping show on the facade. [GO TOKYO — Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/spot/18/index.html)
- **Shinjuku Gyoen** — ¥500; **closed Mondays** (Day 7 is a Wednesday, so open); Japanese/English/French garden sections; mid-to-late November autumn foliage. [Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden — official (env.go.jp)](https://www.env.go.jp/garden/shinjukugyoen/english/index.html)
- **Imperial Palace East Gardens** — free; **closed Mondays & Fridays** (Day 3 optional add is a Saturday, so open). [Imperial Household Agency — East Gardens](https://www.kunaicho.go.jp/e-event/higashigyoen02.html)

## Food
- **Omoide Yokocho ("Memory Lane")** — lantern-lit alley of tiny yakitori/motsu counters by Shinjuku West Exit; no reservations. [GO TOKYO — Omoide Yokocho](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/spot/537/index.html), [Tokyo Cheapo](https://tokyocheapo.com/food-and-drink/omoide-yokocho/)
- **Fuunji (Yoyogi)** — chicken-and-niboshi tsukemen; huge queues by midday, large noodle size free; tiny cash-friendly counter near Shinjuku south side. [Best Tsukemen in Tokyo 2026 (Tokyo Tour)](https://tokyo-tour.com/blog/best-tsukemen-tokyo), [Standard Japan ramen guide](https://thestandardjapan.com/food/best-ramen-tokyo)
- **Onigiri Asakusa Yadoroku** — Tokyo's oldest onigiri specialist (since 1954), Michelin Bib Gourmand; small counter, limited hours, can sell out. [Michelin Guide listing](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/tokyo-region/tokyo/restaurant/onigiri-asakusa-yadoroku), [official site](http://onigiriyadoroku.com/english.html), [Time Out Tokyo](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/restaurants/onigiri-asakusa-yadoroku)
- **Asakusa Imahan** — century-old sukiyaki/gyukatsu house near Senso-ji (bigger sit-down alternative to Yadoroku). [Asakusa guide (tokyotourism.org)](https://www.tokyotourism.org/asakusa-guide/)
- **Ichiran (Kabukicho branch)** — solo-booth tonkotsu chain, accepts cards/IC/PayPay, some branches open late/24h. [Explore Tokyo's Ramen — Shinjuku 2026 (NAVITIME)](https://japantravel.navitime.com/en/area/jp/guide/NTJnews0042-en/)
- **Ebisu Yokocho** — indoor alley of 20+ independent izakaya (yakitori, oden, fresh fish, wine), ~2-min walk from Ebisu Station East Exit; ~¥2,000+/head, no bookings, group-friendly. [GO TOKYO — Ebisu Yokocho](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/spot/451/index.html), [Kanpai-Japan](https://www.kanpai-japan.com/tokyo/ebisu-yokocho)
- **Toyosu Market** — the working fish market that replaced Tsukiji's inner market; public viewing decks over the tuna-auction area + fresh sushi restaurants; short walk from teamLab Planets. [japan-guide.com — Toyosu Market](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3021_002.html)

## Bars & nightlife
- **Golden Gai** — ~200 micro-bars across six alleys, liveliest after 20:00. **Albatross** (3 floors, chandelier-lit, foreigner-friendly, ¥500 cover w/ snack) is the recommended starter; **Bar Plastic Model** and **Cambiare** are English-friendly. [Tokyo Cheapo — Golden Gai guide](https://tokyocheapo.com/food-and-drink/quick-guide-golden-gai/), [byFood — best Golden Gai bars](https://www.byfood.com/blog/tokyo/best-golden-gai-bars), [Japan Apparently — foreigner-friendly bars 2026](https://japanapparently.com/en/guides/golden-gai-foreigner-friendly-18-bars/)
- **Nonbei Yokocho ("Drunkard's Alley")** — tiny two-storey bars/yakitori counters beside the Shibuya train tracks; smaller, more local cousin of Golden Gai. [GO TOKYO — Nonbei Yokocho](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/spot/533/index.html)
- **The SG Club (Shibuya, Jinnan)** — Shingo Gokan bar, 9 consecutive years on Asia's 50 Best Bars; casual "Guzzle" upstairs (walk-in friendly) + "Sip" speakeasy below; ~10–15 min walk from Shibuya Station. [50Best Discovery — The SG Club](https://www.theworlds50best.com/discovery/Establishments/Japan/Tokyo/The-SG-Club.html), [En Primeur Club](https://www.enprimeurclub.com/bars/the-sg-club)
- **Bar High Five (Ginza)** — Hidetsugu Ueno's no-menu omakase cocktail bar, intimate, world-renowned; reservation advised + cover charge. [Tokyo Nightlife 2026 (E-Housing)](https://e-housing.jp/post/top-5-bars-in-tokyo-2026-guide-best-cocktail-bars-hidden-speakeasies-and-nightlife-hotspots), [Wanderlog — best Tokyo bars](https://wanderlog.com/list/geoCategory/16/best-bars-and-drinks-in-tokyo)
- **Bar Zoetrope (Nishi-Shinjuku)** — 300+ Japanese whiskies, silent-film projections; ~17:00–23:15, closed Sundays (& irregular Mondays). [Time Out Tokyo — Zoetrope](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/bars-and-pubs/zoetrope)
- **Bar Benfiddich (Nishi-Shinjuku)** — Hiroyasu Kayama's foraged-botanical cocktails made tableside; 9 seats; 18:00–03:00 Mon–Sat, closed Sundays; regular 50 Best fixture. [Falstaff — Bar Benfiddich](https://www.falstaff.com/en/bars/bar-benfiddich), [Yelp (hours, updated 2026)](https://www.yelp.com/biz/%E3%83%90%E3%83%BC-%E3%83%99%E3%83%B3%E3%83%95%E3%82%A3%E3%83%87%E3%82%A3%E3%83%83%E3%82%AF-%E6%96%B0%E5%AE%BF%E5%8C%BA)
- **New York Bar (Park Hyatt, 52F)** — the *Lost in Translation* bar; skyline views + live jazz; cover charge (~¥2,500) after ~20:00 (Sun 19:00); smart dress enforced. [Park Hyatt official — New York Bar](https://restaurants.tokyo.park.hyatt.co.jp/en/newyorkbar/), [Truly Tokyo](https://trulytokyo.com/new-york-bar/)

## Neighbourhoods
- **Shibuya / Harajuku (Takeshita-dori, Cat Street), Scramble Crossing, Hachiko** — the west-Yamanote fashion/streetwear spine walkable north-to-south from Meiji Jingu. [Tokyo Cheapo — Shibuya→Harajuku→Meiji Jingu walk](https://tokyocheapo.com/itineraries/shibuya-to-harajuku-meiji-jingu-walking-tour/)
- **Shimokitazawa** — indie/vintage district, 7 min from Shinjuku (Odakyu/Keio Inokashira); no chain stores on main lanes, most thrift shops of any district; **Chicago**, **Flamingo**, **New York Joe Exchange** (in a former bathhouse, 50%-off first Sunday monthly). [The Standard Japan — Shimokitazawa guide 2026](https://thestandardjapan.com/guide/shimokitazawa-neighborhood-guide), [Tokyo Cheapo — Shimokitazawa](https://tokyocheapo.com/locations/west-tokyo/shimokitazawa-2/)
- **Nakameguro** — canal neighbourhood; Meguro River promenade, design shops/galleries, the multi-floor **Starbucks Reserve Roastery** flagship. [GO TOKYO — Nakameguro](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/destinations/southern-tokyo/nakameguro/index.html), [The Standard Japan — Nakameguro guide 2026](https://thestandardjapan.com/guide/nakameguro-neighborhood-guide)

## Day trip — Hakone
- **Odakyu Romancecar Shinjuku → Hakone-Yumoto** — direct limited express, ~85 min; reserved seats (right side C/D for Mt Fuji); leave Shinjuku by ~08:00. [Odakyu official — Hakone](https://odakyu-global.com/destinations/hakone/), [LIVE JAPAN — Romancecar guide](https://livejapan.com/en/in-tokyo/in-pref-kanagawa/in-hakone_odawara/article-a0003297/)
- **Hakone Freepass** — 2/3-day pass covering the mountain railway, cablecar, Owakudani ropeway, Lake Ashi pirate ship and buses; buy digitally (EMot app / Klook) to skip the queue. [Odakyu official — Hakone Freepass](https://odakyu-global.com/passes/hakone-freepass/)
- **The loop: Gora → Sounzan → Owakudani (black eggs, volcanic vents) → Togendai → Lake Ashi pirate ship → Moto-Hakone (Hakone Shrine lake torii)** — glass-sided gondolas, excellent November foliage; ropeway can close for wind/volcanic gas, with substitute buses on the same route. [Hakone Navi — one-day model course](https://www.hakonenavi.jp/international/en/course/244), [Tokyo Cheapo — Tokyo to Hakone](https://tokyocheapo.com/travel/tokyo-to-hakone/)
- **Backup: Hakone Open-Air Museum** — all-weather (indoor galleries + Picasso pavilion) anchor near Gora. [Hakone Open-Air Museum — official](https://www.hakone-oam.or.jp/en/)

## Final-days options (Day 9)
- **Yanaka / Yanesen (Yanaka–Nezu–Sendagi)** — surviving shitamachi old-town district; walk Nippori Station → Yanaka Ginza shopping street → Nezu Shrine (red-torii corridor) → Sendagi; Nippori is ~5 min on the JR Yamanote, so it's an easy low-key half-day. [Magical Trip — Yanaka Ginza guide 2026](https://www.magical-trip.com/media/yanaka-ginza-guide-experience-the-local-life-in-tokyos-old-neighborhood/)
- **Kamakura day trip** — JR Yokosuka Line direct from Tokyo Station, ~1 hr, ~¥920 one way; **Great Buddha at Kotoku-in** (11.3 m bronze, cast 1252, enter the statue for ¥50) + **Hase-dera** (5-min walk apart, reached via the Enoden line from Kamakura Station). [Truly Tokyo — Kamakura day-trip itinerary](https://trulytokyo.com/kamakura-day-trip-itinerary/), [Japan Specialty Traveler — Kamakura/Kotoku-in access](https://japanspecialtraveler.com/guides/kanagawa-kamakura-guide/)

## Airport transfers
- **Narita Express (N'EX)** — direct Shinjuku ↔ Narita, ~80 min; reserved seating. **Haneda** — Toei/Keikyu via Shinagawa, or Airport Limousine bus from Shinjuku (~45–75 min). [JR East — Narita Express](https://www.jreast.co.jp/multi/en/nex/), [Tokyo Airport Limousine — official](https://webservice.limousinebus.co.jp/web/en/)

## Notes / judgement calls made while building this plan
1. **Dates confirmed from flight data, not guessed:** the arrival (Thu 12 Nov, Shinkansen from Kyoto) and the *staggered* departures (Jai — Fri 20 Nov, Haneda 13:15; Mandeep & Tom — Sat 21 Nov, Narita 19:30) are taken from the flight details already in `japan_holiday_updated.html`. The plan's tail (Days 9–10) reflects that split rather than a single generic "departure day." **Note:** the HTML's Tokyo date skeleton and Overview tab mislabel some weekdays (e.g. "Wed 12 Nov", "Thu 20 Nov") — against the real 2026 calendar (9 Nov = Mon, per the Kyoto plan) the correct pairings are **Thu 12, Fri 20, Sat 21**. The rebuilt Tokyo section uses the correct labels; the Overview "Wed 12 – Fri 21" line was corrected to "Thu 12 – Sat 21".
2. **Wednesday-closure venues** (teamLab Planets, Tsukiji Outer Market) were deliberately placed on non-Wednesday days (Thu 19 and Sat 14). The plan still tells the user to verify against each venue's published **November 2026** calendar, because the specific closed Wednesdays vary year to year and aren't yet published far enough ahead to pin here.
3. **Shinjuku Gyoen (closed Mon), Imperial Palace East Gardens (closed Mon/Fri), Tokyo National Museum (closed Mon)** — all checked against the assigned day-of-week and confirmed open.
4. **Sunday-closed bars** (Benfiddich, Zoetrope, High Five) were kept off the Sunday (Day 4) and put on the Wednesday cocktail crawl (Day 7) where all three are open.
5. **Exact opening hours/prices that shift** (Meiji Jingu sunrise/sunset hours, Ichiran branch hours, seasonal teamLab maintenance days) are described as ranges or flagged "verify on booking" rather than pinned to a single number, to stay within the NO SLOP rule.
6. No venue was included that couldn't be verified against at least one current source above — following the precedent of the "Izakaya Itokichi" removal logged in `Kyoto_Itinerary_Sources.md`.
