# Tokyo Accommodation Guide (Updated)

## Trip Details
- **Travellers:** 3 males (26 years old) from the UK
- **Dates in Tokyo:** 8 November – 21 November (13 nights)
- **Budget:** **Maximum £450 per person for the entire stay**
- **Total accommodation budget:** ~£1,350 (£450 × 3)
- **Requirements:**
  - Private accommodation (no hostels)
  - **En-suite/private bathroom**
  - 3 separate beds preferred (or 2 beds + sofa bed)
  - Easy access to the Yamanote Line
  - Close to nightlife, food and transport

- Private en-suite ✔
- Washing machine
- Excellent location
- Around £1,200–£1,350 total if booked early

## Option 2 – Shinjuku Granbell Hotel
- En-suite ✔
- Rooftop bar
- Walk to Golden Gai
- Around £1,100–£1,300 total

## Option 3 – APA Hotel Shinjuku Kabukicho Chuo
- En-suite ✔
- Small rooms but excellent value
- Approximately £900–£1,150 total

## Option 4 – Airbnb Apartment (Entire Apartment)
Look for:
- Entire apartment
- Private bathroom
- 3 beds
- Kabukicho / Higashi-Shinjuku

Target nightly price:
- Under **£100/night**
- 13 nights ≈ £1,300 total
