1:00 pm~2 hrs
Fushimi Inari Taisha
Iconic thousand red torii gates. Hike up — it gets quieter the higher you go. Great city views from the top. Open 24hrs, free entry.
📍 Fushimi — 5 min walk from JR from Kyoto Stn 🍜 Ramen Sen-no-Kaze (quick lunch)
3:30 pm~1 hr
Kenninji Temple / Higashiyama
Kyoto's oldest Zen temple. Twin dragon ceiling painting is a must (interior fee ¥600). Closes 4:30 PM — don't arrive late. Explore Kiyomizu-dera & the old streets nearby.
