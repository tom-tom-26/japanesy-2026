# Sources — Kyoto Itinerary Plan

Every restaurant, bar, temple, and logistics claim in `Kyoto_Itinerary_Plan` was checked against the sources below before being included. Compiled 22 July 2026.

## Temples, gardens & viewpoints
- **Fushimi Inari Taisha** — free entry, open 24 hrs. [Kyoto Station transfer info](https://www.kyotostation.com/traveling-from-kyoto-station-to-inari/); [JR Nara Line timing (5 min, local trains only)](https://www.trip.com/transport/station/fushimi-inari-station-to-kyoto-station.html)
- **Kiyomizu-dera** — ¥500 adult, 6:00–18:00 daily (18:30 in Jul/Aug). [2026 hours & fee guide](https://onedayawaytravel.com/kiyomizudera-tickets/), [charcotrip.com guide](https://charcotrip.com/en/kiyomizu-dera-temple-kyoto/)
- **Kenninji Temple** — ¥800 general admission, 10:00–17:00 (last entry 16:30), Twin Dragons ceiling painting. [japan-guide.com](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3973.html), [gltjp.com hours/fee](https://www.gltjp.com/en/directory/item/11958/)
- **Tenryu-ji Garden (Arashiyama)** — ¥500 garden, 8:30–17:00 (7:30 during Nov 15–30 foliage season). [Tenryu-ji hours/fees](https://www.jrpass.com/blog/tenryu-ji-temple)
- **Ryoan-ji** — ¥600, 8:00–17:00. **Kinkaku-ji** — ¥500, 9:00–17:00. 18-min walk apart. [japan-guide.com Ryoanji](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3909.html), [jeepe.jp Kinkakuji](https://www.jeepe.jp/en/articles/kinkakuji-3)
- **Shogunzuka Seiryuden** — ¥500, panoramic Kyoto viewpoint, subtemple of Shoren-in. [insidekyoto.com](https://www.insidekyoto.com/shoren-shogunzuka-seiryuden-viewpoint-southern-higashiyama), [kanpai-japan.com](https://www.kanpai-japan.com/kyoto/shogunzuka)
- **Murin-an Garden** (Okazaki/Nanzen-ji area, mentioned as an optional add) — built 1894–98 by Yamagata Aritomo, National Place of Scenic Beauty. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murin-an), [kanpai-japan.com](https://www.kanpai-japan.com/kyoto/murin-an)
- **Kyoto International Manga Museum** — ¥900, 10:00–17:00, **closed Wednesdays** (this closure is why it was moved off Day 3 in the plan). [kyotomm.jp official hours](https://kyotomm.jp/en/opentime-cost/)
- **Nishiki Market** — open to ~18:00; many individual stalls close Sundays/Wednesdays; best visited before 10–11am. [japancheapo.com](https://japancheapo.com/entertainment/nishiki-market-kyoto/), [ticketsinjapan.com](https://www.ticketsinjapan.com/media/nishiki-market-opening-hours/)

- **Gion Duck Noodles** — duck tsukemen/ramen, 4.5/5 Tripadvisor. [Tabelog listing](https://tabelog.com/en/kyoto/A2601/A260301/26031755/), [5amramen.com](https://www.5amramen.com/post/gion-duck-noodles-kyoto)
- **Sagano (Yudofu, Arashiyama)** — tofu kaiseki, no reservations. [Tabelog "Best Tofu/Yuba" list](https://tabelog.com/en/kyoto/A2601/A260403/rstLst/RC019910/), [Tabelog Sagano listing](https://tabelog.com/en/kyoto/A2601/A260403/26000814/)
- **Vermillion Cafe** — near Fushimi Inari, open 8:30am–3pm. [Coffeevine review](https://thecoffeevine.com/reviews/cafes/japan/kyoto/vermillion-araki-shrine-kyoto-jp/), [Quinnie's Odyssey](https://quinniesodyssey.com/en/vermillion-cafe-kyoto-en/)
- **Kichi Kichi Omurice** — 14 seats, reservation essential, Chef Motokichi Yukimura. [Tabelog listing](https://tabelog.com/en/kyoto/A2601/A260301/26000226/), [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g298564-d3788825-Reviews-The_Youshokuya_Kichi_Kichi-Kyoto_Kyoto_Prefecture_Kinki.html)
- **Gion Tanto** — okonomiyaki (not ramen — corrected from the earlier draft), Gion Shijo, regularly has a queue. [Tabelog listing](https://tabelog.com/en/kyoto/A2601/A260301/26007590/), [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g298564-d1857387-Reviews-or60-Gion_Tanto-Kyoto_Kyoto_Prefecture_Kinki.html)
- **% Arabica Kyoto Arashiyama** — global flagship store, riverside. [insidekyoto.com](https://www.insidekyoto.com/arabica-arashiyama), [arabica.com official](https://arabica.com/en/location/arabica-kyoto-arashiyama/)
- **Ogawa Coffee** — Kyoto roastery founded 1952. [Kyoto city heritage feature](https://kyomachiya.city.kyoto.lg.jp/en/utilize/ogawacoffee-en/)
- **Tea Ceremony Kyoto Nagomi (Arashiyama)** — English-friendly, prepaid booking. [Official site](https://arashiyama.kyoto-nagomi-tea.com/)

## Bars
- **L'Escamoteur** — magician-themed cocktail bar, Kiyamachi-dori, 8pm–2am. [Japan Times feature](https://www.japantimes.co.jp/life/2023/03/05/food/lescamoteur-kyoto-cocktail-bar-magic/)
- **Bee's Knees** — Prohibition-style speakeasy, Asia's 50 Best Bars #76, walk-in only, closed Sundays. [50 Best Discovery](https://www.theworlds50best.com/discovery/Establishments/Japan/Kyoto/Bees-Knees.html), [Tabelog](https://tabelog.com/en/kyoto/A2601/A260201/26031299/)
- **Bar Rocking Chair** — machiya cocktail bar, bartender Kenji Tsubokura (World Bartender of the Year, IBA). [50 Best Discovery](https://www.theworlds50best.com/discovery/Establishments/Japan/Kyoto/Bar-Rocking-Chair.html)
- **Nokishita711** — reservation-only "liquid cuisine" tasting bar, seats 4. [Atlas Obscura](https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/nokishita711-liquid-cuisine-kyoto), [50 Best Discovery](https://www.theworlds50best.com/discovery/Establishments/Japan/Kyoto/Nokishita-711.html)

## Corrections made from the original draft plan
1. **"Ramen Sen-no-Kaze"** was listed as a quick lunch near Fushimi Inari/Kyoto Station — it's actually located in Kawaramachi/Shinkyogoku, a different part of the city, so it didn't fit the arrival-day lunch logistically. Replaced with **Kyoto Ramen Koji** at the station itself. [Tabelog Sen no Kaze listing](https://tabelog.com/en/kyoto/A2601/A260202/26016307/)
2. **"Gion Tanto"** was tagged as a ramen dinner option — Tabelog classifies it as okonomiyaki, not ramen. Label corrected.
3. **"Izakaya Itokichi"** could not be verified as an existing Gion venue across Tabelog, Tripadvisor, or general search — dropped rather than guessed at, per the zero-hallucination rule.
4. **Kenninji** was scheduled right after an afternoon Fushimi Inari visit, but it closes for entry at 16:30 — moved to Day 3 late morning where the timing comfortably works.
5. **Kyoto International Manga Museum** was implicitly grouped with Nishiki Market on Wed 11 Nov — the museum is closed every Wednesday. Moved to Day 4 (Thursday) as an optional add.
6. Confirmed the day-of-week labels already in the spreadsheet/HTML (Mon 9 Nov, Tue 10, Wed 11, Thu 12) are correct for **2026**, not 2025 — the HTML's title/hero text still said "Nov 2025," which appears to be a leftover typo, now corrected.
